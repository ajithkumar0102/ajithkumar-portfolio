Learning types

Supervised learning predicts labels (classification, regression). Unsupervised learning finds structure such as clusters. Reinforcement learning learns from rewards.

Model workflow

Collect and clean data, choose features, split into train and test sets, train, then evaluate with accuracy, precision and recall. Watch for overfitting.

Neural networks

Layers of neurons apply weights and activation functions. Backpropagation adjusts the weights to reduce error. CNNs are the standard choice for images.

Computer vision

Images are arrays of pixels. Filtering, contrast and colour correction, and wavelet fusion are the ideas behind my underwater enhancement project.

Generative AI & LLMs

Large language models use transformers to predict the next token. Prompting, context and tool use shape how AI assistants and agents behave.

Responsible AI

Biased data gives biased models, so data quality, fairness and privacy matter as much as accuracy.
